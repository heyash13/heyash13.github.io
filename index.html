Research & Manuscripts Under Review

1. REFLEX: Residual-Stream Steering of Refusal Representations in LLMs ACM Under Review
Under peer review at ACM (2024)
Investigates the causal representation of refusal mechanisms in frontier large language models. We demonstrate that refusal is encoded as low-rank directional activations across intermediate residual stream layers. We introduce REFLEX, an activation-guidance framework that isolates and steers these vectors during inference to prevent adversarial jailbreaks without model retraining.
2. The Confused Deputy in the Swarm: Cross-IPC Privilege Escalation and Memory Poisoning in Multi-Agent Systems ACM Under Review
Under peer review at ACM (2024)
Analyzes isolation failures and access-control vulnerabilities across inter-process communication (IPC) channels in collaborative, multi-agent LLM systems. Demonstrates how untrusted agent memory state can poison shared context vectors and induce unauthorized tool execution across trust boundaries.
3. How Evaluation Rewards Causally Steer Automated LLM Fuzzing and Distort Vulnerability Discovery ACM Under Review
Under peer review at ACM (2024)
Examines the impact of metric and reward function designs in automated LLM-driven fuzzers. Shows that optimization objectives centered purely on branch coverage introduce severe search biases, leading fuzzers to miss deep semantic memory corruption bugs while repeatedly triggering superficial faults.
